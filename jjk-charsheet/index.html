Press Bonus Action
When ending your turn adjacent to a target, press them. Their next attack suffers a penalty equal to your highest stat to damage and −2 to hit anyone other than you, but deals +2 damage and +1 to hit you. Only one person may press a single target. Effect ends if the target moves more than 50 ft. away.
Block Reaction  ·  Power
Roll your raw Power stat against the incoming roll to hit. Meet or beat it to halve the damage. On failure, take full damage.
Dodge Reaction  ·  Speed
Roll your raw Speed stat and beat the incoming roll to hit to avoid completely — negating all damage and side effects. On success you may also move up to 5 ft. in any direction. On failure, take 1.5× damage.
Leap Reaction  ·  Reflexes
For attacks within 15 ft., roll your Reflexes substat against the attack's roll to hit minus your highest of PL or SL. Meet or beat it to leap in front of the attack and shield an ally, taking the damage yourself. You may still react to the attack but cannot Dodge it. Once per turn.
Cursed Energy Coating Reaction  ·  Technique
Coat yourself in cursed energy to reduce incoming damage. Roll a Talent check against a DC equal to your Technique Level x 2. On success, reduce damage by your base Imbue or Output value (whichever is higher). You can spend additional CE to stack the coating for greater reduction — each stack costs 3 CE and raises the DC by 2, adding 1d4 extra reduction per stack. Max stacks scale with Technique Level: TL 1–2 = 1 stack, TL 3–4 = 2 stacks, TL 5–6 = 3 stacks.
